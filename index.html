Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sholiscalc app ("the app") handles information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any information on a server. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

Internet access

The app does not connect to the internet. It works entirely offline.

Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions.

Data stored on your device

Your shopping list is saved locally on your device so it's there the next time you open the app. This data is never transmitted anywhere and is not accessible to us. Calculator entries are not saved at all once the app is closed.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this locally stored data.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
